why we need hashable and not hashable types in python??


The central idea : 

Dictionary key must stay stable
            ↓
Python needs a stable hash
            ↓
Every part of the key must be hashable


eg : 
("Sanjay", 25)        # Hashable: all parts are fixed
("Sanjay", (80, 90))  # Hashable: nested tuple is also fixed
("Sanjay", [80, 90])  # Not hashable: the list can change
